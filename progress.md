- Colour findings for 5.1: the plain swscale shader (Remotion's) is 0.034 to 0.05 FLIP from HyperFrames' pixels, because HyperFrames takes frames through a lossy JPEG hop; the hop emulation takes the GPU output to 97 to 99% exact on 1080p video frames and 63 to 81% exact on dense textures (mean abs 0.2 to 0.3). Stage A matches `gbrp` exactly. Stage B (rgb to yuv420) still has about 0.2% of samples off by one (swscale rounds through a limited-range 15-bit intermediate; not modelled), which flips a few quantiser decisions on dense texture. ffmpeg's default mjpeg DCT is islow with PASS1_BITS 1 (`-dct int` is 4), quant steps are the written table, bias 0.375, ties round up only where Q is 4 or 8, DC is half-up.
